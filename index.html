Selected Projects

01WEB
VISUALIZATION

Singapore MRT Visualization

Personal project · Interactive web application

An interactive visualization of Singapore's rail network, with station search, 2D/3D views, camera navigation, and client-side train and arrival simulation.

Technologies: React, MapLibre GL, MapTiler, Turf.js

Live demo (opens in a new tab)

Technical notes

Rail GeoJSON is organized for rendering and station selection. Turf.js supports route distances, positions and bearings. Train movements and arrivals are simulated in the browser; they are not live service data.

023D
SPATIAL DATA

Metro Station Spatial Reconstruction In progress

Personal project · Yau Tong Station

Exploring a 3D reconstruction of Yau Tong Station, with a longer-term interest in digital twins and interactive station environments.

Focus: Spatial data, 3D reconstruction, environment modeling

Project notes

This ongoing study investigates how station data can inform a navigable 3D environment. Current challenges include continuous floors, coherent walls and natural connections where source data is incomplete.

03AR
ANIMATION

AR Flower Growth

Personal project · Mobile augmented reality

A flower-pot recognition experience that introduces a virtual plant into the real world and reveals its growth through staged animation.

Technologies: Unity, Blender, Android

Technical notes

The experience connects recognition with an animation state machine. Development explored baking Blender procedural animation to bones for use in a mobile application.

04AR
HERITAGE

AR Cultural Heritage

Collaborative project

A mobile AR project exploring cultural artifact display through image recognition and the gradual appearance of 3D models, developed within a three-person team.

Technologies: Unity URP, Vuforia, Android

Project notes

The project involved an Android AR experience, a welcome interface and a stylized visual direction for artifact presentation.
