SINGAPORE / RAIL NETWORKINTERACTIVE SPATIAL VISUALIZATION ↗
PERSONAL PROJECT · WEBLive project

Singapore MRT Visualization

An interactive exploration of Singapore's rail network, with station search, 2D/3D views, camera navigation and client-side train simulation.

ReactMapLibre GLTurf.js
Open live project ↗ (opens in a new tab)
Read project notes

Rail GeoJSON is organized for map rendering and station selection. Turf.js supports route distances, positions and bearings. Trains and arrivals are simulated in the browser; they do not represent live service data.

YAU TONG / SPATIAL EXPLORATION
3D ENVIRONMENT STUDY
PERSONAL PROJECT · 3DIn progress

Metro Station Spatial Reconstruction

Exploring a 3D reconstruction of Yau Tong Station, with a longer-term interest in digital twins and interactive station environments.

Spatial data3D reconstructionDigital twins
Read project notes

This ongoing study investigates how station data can inform a navigable 3D environment. Current challenges include continuous floors, coherent walls and natural connections where the source data is incomplete.

AR Flower Growth

A flower-pot recognition experience that brings a virtual plant into the real world, revealing its growth through staged animation.

UnityBlenderAndroid
Read project notes

The experience connects recognition with an animation state machine. The development explored baking Blender procedural animation to bones for use in a mobile application.
